Department Overview

Welcome to Automobile Engineering

Established in 1996, the Automobile Engineering Department at MSPVL provides students with comprehensive training in production, service automation, vehicle design, and modern automotive systems. We bridge the gap between classroom theory and automotive industry practice.

Automobile Engineering blends mechanical, electrical, electronic, software, and safety engineering principles as applied to the design, manufacture, and operation of all types of vehicles — from motorcycles to commercial trucks.

Scope After Diploma in Automobile Engineering

India's automotive sector is the 4th largest in the world — and growing rapidly. Automobile Engineering graduates from MSPVL are placed in top automotive brands and manufacturing companies every year.

  • Production / Quality Engineer in automobile manufacturing plants
  • Vehicle inspection officer in RTO and transport authorities
  • Lateral entry to B.E. Automobile / Mechanical Engineering
  • Entrepreneur — own automotive service workshop / dealership
