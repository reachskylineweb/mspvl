Department Overview

Welcome to Civil Engineering

Established in 1985, the Civil Engineering department is one of the founding pillars of MSPVL. The department focuses on professional practice of designing and developing infrastructure — from roads and bridges to buildings and water treatment plants.

Civil engineering deals with the design, construction, and maintenance of the physical and naturally built environment. Our graduates are instrumental in public works such as roads, bridges, dams, airports, and structural buildings.

Career Opportunities

Civil engineers are essential to every development project in India. Government bodies, construction firms, and consultancies actively recruit our graduates.

  • Site Engineer / Project Supervisor in construction
  • Structural Draughtsman in engineering consultancies
  • Government: PWD, TWAD, TNHB, Highways Dept.
  • Lateral entry to B.E. Civil Engineering
  • Freelance estimation and construction consultant
