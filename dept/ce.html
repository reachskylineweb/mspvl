Department Overview

Welcome to Computer Engineering

Established in 2001, the Computer Engineering department aims to match the proper hardware and software to meet the technological, scientific, and administrative needs of businesses and industries. Our curriculum covers both core computing concepts and emerging technologies.

Computer Engineering integrates multiple fields of computer science and electronic engineering required to develop computer hardware and software — programmes, networks, and systems alike.
