Department Overview

Welcome to EEE Department

Established in 1997, the EEE department creates students with strong theoretical knowledge, good practical exposure, critical thinking and innovation. We cover everything from power generation to cutting-edge electronics and automation systems.

EEE is one of the most versatile engineering disciplines, enabling graduates to work across power utilities, manufacturing automation, consumer electronics, and the fast-growing renewable energy sector.
