Department Overview

Welcome to Mechanical Engineering

Established in 1985, the Mechanical Engineering Department at MSPVL has a rich legacy of producing industry-ready engineers. Our goal is to prepare students for readily employable positions in major MNCs by imparting core technical knowledge.

Mechanical Engineering is an engineering branch that combines physics, mathematics, and materials science to design, analyze, manufacture, and maintain mechanical systems — one of the oldest and broadest engineering disciplines.
