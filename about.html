Established in 1985

The M.S.P.V.L. Polytechnic College was established by the Thiru M.S.P.V. Nadar-Lakshmithai Ammal Educational Trust in 1985. The trust was formed to promote technical education in memory of the Late Shri M.S.P. Velayutha Nadar, a leading industrialist and philanthropist.

Over four decades, we have evolved from a small institute to a premier polytechnic with ultra-modern laboratories, sprawling workshops, and world-class sports arenas.
