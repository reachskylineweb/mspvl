Department Overview

The Mechanical Engineering department, established in 1985, is one of the pillars of MSPVL. We focus on thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, CAD/CAM, and advanced manufacturing processes.

Our students are well-prepared for leading MNCs like TVS, Hyundai, and Ashok Leyland through intensive industrial training.
